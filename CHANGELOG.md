# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- No changes in the game. Released again with DibsUI-Options 0.2.1 and DibsUI 0.3.2

## 0.1.0

- First beta: party frames in DibsUI's style that replace Blizzard's. Each member has an orb with their portrait (or class icon) and a ring for their power, a class-colored health bar with heal and absorb prediction, their name, level, leader, role, raid marker and ready check
- Party pets get a small bar under their owner's health bar, with the pet's name on mouseover
- Your buffs on each member under their frame, and all their debuffs beside it, dispellable ones first. Masque can skin both
- Members out of range fade to 30%
- Layouts: vertical growing up or down, or horizontal growing left, right or from the center, with scale and bar width sliders. You can show yourself in the party too
- Test frames to set everything up alone. Unlock frames in the DibsUI window to drag them
- Settings are the Party Frames tab of the DibsUI window: type `/dui party`. They're saved in the shared DibsUI profiles
- With DibsUI installed, the party frames use its master font and last names
- Blizzard's party frames and its raid frame manager tab are hidden while you're in a party. In a raid, Blizzard's raid frames take over
- Needs DibsUI-Options 0.2.0
