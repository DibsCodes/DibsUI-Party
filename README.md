# DibsUI-Party

Party frames for World of Warcraft: Forever, made to match [DibsUI](https://github.com/DibsCodes/DibsUI-forever).

This addon is in early development.

## Dependencies

- **[DibsUI-Options](https://github.com/DibsCodes/dibsui-options)** (required): the settings window and profiles shared by the DibsUI addons. The party frames' settings are its **Party Frames** tab. Type `/dui party` to open it, or open the window with `/dui` or the minimap button.
- **DibsUI** (optional): the party frames work without it. With it, they follow DibsUI's master font and last names, and DibsUI's Unlock frames button moves them too.
- **Masque** (optional): skins the buff and debuff icons.

## Install

Copy the `DibsUI-Party` and `DibsUI-Options` folders into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.
